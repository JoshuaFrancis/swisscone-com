The cones

Three cones. One recipe. Sixty years of proof.

The Classic

The original 1962 formula. Brown butter, raw Swiss honey, alpine milk. Pressed at 185°C for exactly 70 seconds. The one that started it.

Dutch cocoa pressed into the Classic formula. For gelato and sorbetto. The contrast of bitter cone and sweet filling is the reason people come back.

The press

Same cast iron. Same batter. Same 70 seconds. Every cone since 1962.

The Swiss Cone press has never been modernised. The cast iron plates were machined in Zurich in 1961 and have produced every cone under this name since. The pattern they press into the batter creates the structural geometry that prevents collapse for up to four minutes after filling.

That is not a marketing claim. It is an engineering property of the lattice. Our clients notice it. Their customers do not, which is the point.
